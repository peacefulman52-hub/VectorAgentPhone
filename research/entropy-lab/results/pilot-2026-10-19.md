# Exploratory pilot result — 2026-10-19

**Status: completed; exploratory only.** GitHub Actions run: https://github.com/peacefulman52-hub/VectorAgentPhone/actions/runs/39263283594

## Setup

- Model: `distilgpt2`, CPU, Transformers 4.57.6, PyTorch 2.14.1+cpu
- Fixed-seed sample: 40 single-token antonym completion items
- Conditions: baseline; activation perturbation against the baseline winning token; random same-norm perturbation; prompt-only uncertainty warning
- Layer: final GPT-2 transformer block (index 5)
- All 40 task items were retained; no candidate tokenization exclusions.

## Results

| Condition | Accuracy | Mean probability of correct candidate | Mean confidence in selected candidate | Brier score | 10-bin ECE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No intervention | 0.20 | 0.336 | 0.734 | 0.485 | 0.534 |
| Targeted baseline-winner suppression | 0.80 | 0.779 | 0.971 | 0.193 | 0.185 |
| Random same-norm intervention | 0.20 | 0.336 | 0.729 | 0.484 | 0.529 |
| Prompt-only uncertainty warning | 0.35 | 0.410 | 0.720 | 0.407 | 0.370 |

Paired accuracy difference vs baseline:
- Targeted suppression: **+0.60**, bootstrap 95% percentile interval **[+0.35, +0.85]**
- Random same-norm intervention: **0.00**, interval **[0.00, 0.00]**
- Prompt-only warning: **+0.15**, interval **[+0.05, +0.275]**

## Interpretation

The targeted perturbation changed the model's next-token distribution and raised accuracy on this particular antonym task. The same-norm random perturbation did not. The model was initially poor at the task and strongly preferred the source word; suppressing its baseline winner often moved probability toward the antonym. This is a plausible task-specific steering effect, not evidence of introspection.

Confidence calibration improved under targeted suppression but remained imperfect: mean confidence in the selected candidate was 0.971 while accuracy was 0.80. The prompt-only warning also changed results, so prompt effects need further controls.

## What this does not show

- It does not show that the model detected or understood the internal intervention.
- It does not test a verbal confidence report.
- It does not establish consciousness, subjective experience, or general introspection.
- The sample is small and uses one model, one task template, one layer, and one intervention scale. The bootstrap interval is descriptive, not a substitute for independent replication.

## Next experiment

Test whether a model's **explicit confidence report** predicts correctness after interventions, using the same task items and preregistered controls. Include sham interventions, several intervention strengths, a second task family, and independent reruns. Keep the report separate from probability-based confidence; compare both against actual correctness.
